Slide 6

🐜 Types of Invertebrates

Invertebrates can look very different!

🦋

Insects

  • 6 legs
  • 3 main body parts
  • Example: butterfly
🕷️

Arachnids

  • 8 legs
  • Example: spider
🐌

Molluscs

  • Soft bodies
  • Some have shells
  • Example: snail
🪱

Worms

  • Long, soft bodies
  • Example: earthworm

💭 Think!

Does an insect have a backbone?

➡️ No! Insects are invertebrates.

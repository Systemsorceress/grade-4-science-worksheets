1️⃣ FIRST STOP

👄 The Mouth

What happens here?

Your teeth chew food into smaller pieces.

Your tongue moves the food around.

Saliva mixes with food and starts breaking it down.

✅ Chewing makes food smaller and easier to digest!
2️⃣ SECOND STOP

🛝 The Oesophagus

The oesophagus is a tube that carries food from your mouth to your stomach.

Muscles squeeze the food downwards.

3️⃣ THIRD STOP

🥣 The Stomach

The stomach is like a mixing machine!

  • Stores food for a while
  • Mixes the food
  • Uses digestive juices
  • Helps break food down
A mixing and breaking-down machine for food!
4️⃣ FOURTH STOP

🌀 Small Intestine

This is a VERY important part of digestion!

The small intestine absorbs most nutrients from our food.

5️⃣ FIFTH STOP

💧 Large Intestine

The large intestine absorbs water from the remaining food mixture.

The remaining material becomes more solid and eventually becomes waste.

🧠 FIX THE MISTAKE

Can You Spot the Mistake?

“The large intestine absorbs most nutrients.”

✅ Correct: The small intestine absorbs most nutrients.
“Food travels directly from the mouth to the stomach.”

✅ Correct: Food travels through the oesophagus before reaching the stomach.

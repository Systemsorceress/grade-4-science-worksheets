Meet the Four Types of Teeth

Humans have four main types of teeth. Each type has a special job.

🦷

Incisors

Cut and bite food.

🦷

Canines

Tear food.

🦷

Premolars

Crush food.

🦷

Molars

Grind food.

💡 Helpful Clues

Remember:

  • Incisors are at the front.
  • Canines are pointed.
  • Premolars are behind canines.
  • Molars are at the back.
